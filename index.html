Terms & cancellation policy

1. Entry terms

18+ only. One entry covers one person and is paired automatically. Non-transferable. Confirmed upon payment.

2. How matching works

Pitch Them Perfect selects matches via your questionnaire responses. Pairing is blind — no advance approval, swaps, or refunds based on match. No romantic outcome is guaranteed.

3. Participant conduct

Respect is required. Zero tolerance for harassment, resulting in immediate removal with no refund. Privacy and consent rules apply throughout.

4. Fitness & readiness

This is a strenuous event. Participants confirm their own fitness and health readiness, and should advise the event team of any health issues before the race.

5. Photography & filming

Event coverage may be used for future marketing. Opt-out is available on request.

6. Cancellation policy

No refunds within 24 hours of the race. 50% refund for earlier cancellation. 100% refund if organizers cancel the event. Cancel by DM to @pitchthemperfect.
